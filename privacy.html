Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy describes how Masiyatunda Co. Ltd handles information when you use masiyatunda.com and the MilkyWay app. For privacy questions, contact masiyatunda@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: email address, username, display name, profile photo, and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Content and communications: posts, photos, videos, comments, messages, live-room requests, and other content you submit or share.
  • Forms and support requests: contact details and information or attachments you include in job, artist-registration, or support forms.
  • Service and device information: technical logs and information such as browser/device details, language, referring page, and app activity needed to operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • Wallet records: app balance and gift activity associated with your account, where those features are used.

How information is used

We use information to provide accounts and app features, publish content you choose to share, deliver messages and live sessions, respond to requests, maintain service security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot or improve the service.

If you use live audio or video, the app requests microphone or camera access for that session. You control whether to grant those permissions in your device settings.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to host and operate the service, including Supabase for account, database, and storage functions; LiveKit for real-time live sessions; and hosting, content-delivery, and security providers. They process information as needed to provide their services to us.

Content you publish publicly may be visible to other users. Information you send in a message or share in a live session is available to its recipients. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the service, or our legal rights.

Storage and retention

The website and app may use local storage, browser storage, or cached files to remember settings, maintain app functionality, and support offline loading. Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect service security. Retention can vary by information type and provider.

Your choices and account deletion

You can update some profile information and app preferences in the app. To request deletion of your MilkyWay account and associated personal information, use our account deletion request page. We may need to verify the request. Some information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes.

Children

MilkyWay is not intended for children under 13 or any higher minimum age required by local law. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current version will be posted on this page with its latest update date. Contact masiyatunda@gmail.com with questions or requests concerning your information.
