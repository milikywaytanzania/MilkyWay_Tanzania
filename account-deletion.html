Request MilkyWay account deletion

You can request deletion of your MilkyWay account and associated personal information by emailing our support team. Send the request from the email address registered to your account when possible, and include your MilkyWay username or profile link so we can identify the account.

Do not include your password. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email an account deletion request

What happens next

We will review the request and may ask you to verify ownership. Once verified, we will process deletion of the account and associated personal information, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes. Sending a request does not delete your account immediately.

For help, email masiyatunda@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.
